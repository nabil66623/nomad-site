Our Identity

Who We Are

Bayt Al Mocha — House of Mocha — is a premium Yemeni coffee experience rooted in the ancient trade routes that gave the world its first cup. We bring Yemen's centuries-old coffee heritage to the modern specialty market.

More than a café. A cultural institution. A statement that the birthplace of coffee deserves its place at the top of the global specialty conversation.

"Yemen gave the world coffee. We give the world Yemen."
03
The Origin

The Story of Mocha

The port city of Mocha, Yemen was the world's first coffee trading hub — the origin of the word "mocha" itself. For centuries, Yemeni farmers cultivated heirloom varietals in the highlands above 1,500 meters, developing flavor profiles unmatched anywhere on earth.

Bayt Al Mocha was built to honor that legacy — sourcing directly from Yemeni farmers and bringing those extraordinary beans to discerning customers worldwide.
